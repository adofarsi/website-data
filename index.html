Ado Farsi

Computational mechanics · Machine learning

I am a Research Fellow (Co‑Investigator) at University College London and a Visiting Researcher at Imperial College London. Alongside my academic work, I am co‑founder of Tanuki Technologies.

My expertise lies in computational mechanics — particularly finite element (FEM) and discrete element (DEM) modelling — as well as machine learning. I have been an invited speaker and session organiser at international conferences including WCCM, ECCOMAS, USNCCM and ARMA.

I work on solid and fluid mechanics, with a focus on Earth materials such as rock and ice, and engineered materials such as concrete and ceramics. My research spans applications from rock mechanics and fibre‑reinforced concrete tunnels to the optimisation of catalyst supports for hydrogen production and the simulation of Arctic sea ice.
