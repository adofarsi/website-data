About me

I was born in Monza, near Milan (Italy), and before moving to the UK I studied at the Polytechnic University of Milan, where I earned a Bachelor's degree in Building Engineering and a Master's degree in Structural Engineering, before completing my PhD in Computational Mechanics at Imperial College London.
